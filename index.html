WHAT IS PELLOWAH?

A deeper connection.
A new possibility.

Pellowah is an energy healing practice with consciousness at its heart — and an invitation to discover more of who you are.

Its purpose is to support a shift in awareness, opening space for change from within. For some, that may mean greater clarity or a renewed sense of purpose. For others, it may be a deeper spiritual connection, or the beginning of an awakening to possibilities they hadn’t yet recognised.

For me, Pellowah became far more than a healing technique. It opened a relationship with myself, with spirituality and with the world around me that I hadn’t known was possible. That is the possibility I love sharing: space to explore your own depth, trust yourself more fully and discover what a meaningful life feels like to you.

You don’t need to arrive with particular beliefs or answers. Your experience, and what it comes to mean, will be your own. Curiosity is enough to begin.

Pellowah was brought into the world by Kachina Ma’an in Australia in 2003. Today, practitioners and teachers share it across the UK and around the globe. I’m grateful to be part of that worldwide community.

MEET SARAH

An unexpected beginning.
A meaningful change.

Before Pellowah, I was a drama teacher. Energy healing wasn’t part of my world. Then the word Pellowah kept finding its way to me, and eventually I booked a course in Peebles.

My first session was during that course. Driving home afterwards, everything felt brighter. I could breathe more freely. I felt uplifted, yet grounded. The next day brought a clarity that stayed with me.

I learned Pellowah Levels I and II with Julie Parker, and later trained and received my teacher attunement directly from Kachina Ma’an, Pellowah’s founder. I feel fortunate to carry their teaching into the work I share with you.

Now, as a Pellowah practitioner and teacher based in Cumbria, I share that opportunity with others. My approach is rooted in service, respect, responsibility and permission — with room for your experience to be entirely your own.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

You may be wondering.

Do I need to have tried energy healing before?

No. You’re welcome to come with no previous experience. Sarah can talk you through what to expect and answer your questions before you decide.

What might I experience in a session?

People describe a range of experiences, including colours, sensations, insights or a sense of stillness. Some notice very little at the time. There is no experience you need to have, and individual responses vary.

Can I receive Pellowah from home?

Yes. Sarah offers distance sessions as well as in-person sessions. Get in touch to discuss a time and the preparation guidance.

Do I need Reiki training or a Pellowah session first?

No. Pellowah was the first energy modality I trained in. Some people try a session before training; others feel curious and come straight to the course. Neither route is required.

Can I book just one day?

I offer the two days together as one complete course. Each day has a different purpose, and they build on each other.

What if I can’t feel energy or don’t understand something?

You don’t need to feel tingling, see colours or have any particular experience. Some people notice a lot; others notice very little. Your experience is your own.

If something doesn’t make sense, ask me. Ask the obvious question, the strange question, or ask me to explain it again. I want learning to feel like a conversation.

Do I have to become a practitioner after training?

No. The course teaches you to facilitate Pellowah, but you can come entirely for your own development. You don’t need to begin offering sessions afterwards.

Is Pellowah another form of Reiki?

No. Pellowah is a separate modality with its own teachings and approach. In my experience, it feels very different too.

Within Pellowah teachings, it is described as 100% pure source energy, operating at a higher frequency than Reiki and working with consciousness and DNA to support change.

The practitioner’s role is to facilitate that energy. We don’t diagnose, scan chakras, sense or interpret blockages, or decide where the energy needs to go. I describe it as being like a clear glass tube, without adding my own energy or interpretation.

Pellowah is completely non-touch, and the session is deliberately quiet: no music, added scents, sound accompaniment or talking during the healing. This gives you space to turn your attention inwards and experience Pellowah for yourself.

Do I need to be spiritual?

No. You don’t need to share my beliefs or use particular language. You’re welcome to question what you hear and explore it for yourself. For me, curiosity is enough.
