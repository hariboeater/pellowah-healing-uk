WHAT IS PELLOWAH?

A shift within.
A different outlook.

Pellowah is a non-touch energy healing practice whose central purpose, within its teachings, is a shift in consciousness.

It approaches you as a whole, rather than asking the practitioner to focus on a particular symptom or decide what needs attention. The invitation is to explore a wider awareness of yourself, your choices and the world around you.

For me, it became a journey of self-discovery and spiritual connection. It opened up possibilities I hadn’t known were there. Other people may find their own meaning in it — through clarity, curiosity, stillness or a changed perspective.

Source energy, DNA and meridians in Pellowah teachings

Pellowah is described within its teachings as 100% pure source energy, working at a very high frequency. The teachings speak of connecting and activating “12 strands of DNA” and unblocking and realigning the body’s energy meridians.

These are concepts within Pellowah’s spiritual framework, rather than established biological mechanisms or evidence that a session changes physical DNA. You’re welcome to explore the philosophy, ask questions and decide what it means to you.

SARAH’S WAY OF EXPLAINING PELLOWAH

The house is a metaphor for possibilities within you that you may not yet have explored.

Pellowah teachings describe consciousness and DNA as capable of more than we normally access. They describe the energy as working at a very high frequency, supporting connections that allow change. You don’t have to accept those ideas as beliefs before you come.

What fascinates me is how intelligent the energy feels. I’m not deciding what you need, and you’re not lying there trying to solve something. My way of describing it is that your consciousness and the Pellowah energy meet, and whatever needs attention seems to come into focus.

That is how I understand the experience. Yours may be different. No two people will explore those rooms in quite the same way.

DURING YOUR SESSION

Nothing to perform.
Space to receive.

For an in-person session, you lie down fully clothed. Pellowah is completely non-touch, and there’s nothing you need to do.

You don’t have to meditate, quiet your mind or try to “receive properly”. The setting is deliberately quiet, with no music, added scents, sound accompaniment or talking during the healing, so you have space to turn your attention inwards.

My role is to facilitate the session. I don’t diagnose, read chakras or interpret blockages. I describe it as a clear glass tube passing through me: within Pellowah teachings, the energy is 100% pure source energy, without my own energy mixed into it or my interpretation directing it.

PERSONAL & SPIRITUAL EXPANSION

What might change?

Sometimes subtle.
Sometimes meaningful in unexpected ways.

A shift in awareness may show up less as a dramatic moment and more as a different way of meeting everyday life. These are possibilities to explore, rather than results you need to achieve.

A wider perspective.

Seeing a situation differently, considering choices you hadn’t noticed, or finding space between a familiar reaction and what you choose next.

A steadier sense of self.

Exploring inner confidence, personal responsibility and the freedom to make decisions that feel more aligned with who you are.

A deeper connection.

For those drawn to spiritual exploration, Pellowah can be a way to reflect on intuition, connection to Source and a broader sense of purpose.

You might notice something during a session, or reflect on differences over the following days and weeks. Experiences vary; no particular change is promised. Pellowah is a complementary practice and does not replace medical or mental health care.

A DIFFERENT APPROACH

Pellowah
and Reiki.

Pellowah is a separate modality with its own teachings. In my experience, it feels very different too.

Pellowah teachings describe its energy as operating at a higher frequency than Reiki. This is a description within that spiritual framework, rather than a measured comparison.

In Pellowah, I don’t scan chakras, interpret blockages or choose where energy should go. The practitioner remains neutral, facilitating the session without adding other modalities.

The quiet, non-touch setting is intentional. With no music, aromas or talking during the healing, there is space to turn inward. Your experience — and what it means — remains yours.
